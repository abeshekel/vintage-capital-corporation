About Us

A focused holding company for ambitious owners, operators, and partners.

Vintage Capital Corporation was formed to acquire, support, and scale companies with strong foundations, differentiated capabilities, and meaningful room to grow.

Who We Are

Built around long-term ownership and disciplined execution.

VCC is a New York-based holding company focused on acquiring and accelerating businesses across healthcare IT, business services, enterprise software, SaaS, automated billing, payroll technology, and Revenue Cycle Management.

Our interest is in businesses that already possess value but have not yet reached their full market potential. We look for strong customer relationships, practical operational use cases, trusted service delivery, and opportunities to expand through better reach, process, technology, and execution.

Our Background

Experience across sectors, geographies, and operating environments.

Healthcare & Medical IT

Experience building and scaling medical IT companies and healthcare technology businesses, including workflow, billing, and technology-enabled services.

Software & Fintech

Background in software businesses, fintech startups, enterprise technology, and commercial infrastructure that supports growth.

Global Perspective

Exposure across the United States, APAC, and the MENA region, including telecommunications infrastructure and national policy formulation.

Purpose

Alongside long-term business initiatives, the firm remains committed to philanthropic and humanitarian initiatives.

Our Philosophy

Protect what makes a business valuable. Strengthen what helps it scale.

We are not interested in change for its own sake. Our objective is to understand what already works, preserve the trust a business has earned, and add the systems, capital, relationships, and operating discipline needed for the next stage.
