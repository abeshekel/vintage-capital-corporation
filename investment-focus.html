Investment Focus

Where essential services, software, and operating complexity create opportunity.

VCC focuses on markets where specialized expertise, workflow importance, and recurring customer needs support durable businesses.

Core Areas

Primary sectors of interest.

AI-Enabled Medical Companies

Healthcare companies using AI to improve clinical workflow, administrative efficiency, patient engagement, documentation, decision support, or revenue operations.

Revenue Cycle Management

RCM providers, medical billing businesses, coding support, denial management, claims workflow, and outsourced revenue operations.

Healthcare IT

Medical software, workflow platforms, practice operations, patient communication, data services, and healthcare infrastructure tools.

Enterprise Software & SaaS

Vertical SaaS, back-office software, workflow automation, compliance tools, reporting systems, and embedded business applications.

Automated Billing Platforms

Recurring billing, payment operations, invoicing automation, subscription management, and financial operations software.

Payroll Technology

Payroll software, workforce operations, benefits-adjacent platforms, compliance workflows, and employer administrative technology.

Business Services

Specialized services that are mission-critical, repeatable, and relationship-driven.

Beyond software and healthcare technology, VCC evaluates business services companies with strong customer trust, repeatable delivery, and clear value to corporate or professional clients.

  • Professional services support
  • Healthcare administration
  • Outsourced operations
  • Compliance-oriented services
  • Technology-enabled support
  • Financial operations services
  • Credentialed advisory support
  • Specialized B2B service providers

Common Attributes

The sectors may differ, but the business quality signals are consistent.

Recurring need

Products or services tied to ongoing operational requirements rather than one-time discretionary demand.

Embedded workflow

Companies that become important to how customers run their business or serve their own clients.

Expansion potential

Opportunities to grow by improving sales reach, partnerships, retention, automation, or service delivery.
