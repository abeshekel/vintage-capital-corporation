Acquisition Criteria

Focused criteria. Flexible structure. Professional process.

We evaluate each opportunity on its own merits, with a preference for differentiated businesses that can grow with stronger commercial reach and operational support.

Ideal Profile

Companies with value already present and upside still unrealized.

01

Small to lower-middle-market companies with high potential and defensible customer value.

02

Healthcare IT, RCM, AI-enabled medical, business services, enterprise software, SaaS, automated billing, payroll technology, or adjacent B2B markets.

03

Founder-owned, family-owned, or closely held businesses where ownership is considering succession, transition, recapitalization, or strategic partnership.

04

Companies constrained by limited market reach, underdeveloped sales infrastructure, operational bandwidth, or insufficient strategic focus.

05

Businesses with repeatable demand, trusted customer relationships, specialized expertise, or workflow importance.

Transaction Situations

We welcome confidential conversations across a range of scenarios.

Owner transition

A founder or family owner seeking a thoughtful path to transition while protecting employees, customers, and legacy.

Growth partnership

A business with strong capabilities that needs help expanding reach, commercial systems, or operational infrastructure.

Platform acquisition

A company that can serve as a foundation for long-term growth in a focused sector.

Add-on acquisition

Specialized businesses that can complement a broader platform strategy in healthcare IT, billing, software, or business services.

Succession planning

Owners looking for a responsible steward when the next generation or internal team is not positioned to take over fully.

Operational reset

Good companies that need sharper process, reporting, sales execution, or technology adoption to move forward.

For Intermediaries
