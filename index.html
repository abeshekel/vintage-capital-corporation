Shahid Mir

Chief Executive Officer

Shahid has spent forty-three years in information technology as an entrepreneur, systems architect, and advisor. He was a founding member of Pakistan’s National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences, now one of the largest computer science universities in South Asia and the Middle East, enrolling 7,000+ students, and served on its Academic Council as a Board of Governors nominee for more than a decade.

As founding CEO of the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB), he helped build the country’s technology export industry from the ground up, leading industry delegations to COMDEX in Las Vegas and CeBIT in Hannover through the mid-1990s. He has advised at head-of-government level, sat on an economic reforms committee and a national IT agenda board, and consulted to USAID, the Asian Development Bank, and the World Bank on programs applying technology to healthcare, education, and agriculture.

As Chief Systems Architect he has run technology and telecommunications programs across industrial sectors, managing project portfolios worth fifty million dollars, and has led teams whose cloud-based work won competition awards. He advises large industrial and banking groups on technology strategy and execution, and has been lead trainer on more than two hundred professional development programs. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering and a Master of Science in Systems Science, with scholarships and high distinctions in both.

Shakeel Akhter

Chief Operating Officer

Shakeel holds a Master of Science in computer science and began his career in 1990 building systems for Delta Shipping — imports and exports management, container handling, and the financial accounting that tied them together. He spent two years teaching computer science at technical institutes and learned the how-to craft of IT as the industry’s shift began.

In 1991 he joined Crescent Investment Bank as Manager of Information Technology and MIS, where he spent seven years building the systems a bank runs on — customer information, loan management, cash management, securities, capital markets, payroll, and financial accounting — across its head office and branch network, then led the in-house conversion of all of them onto a new database platform. He went on to serve as Vice President of Systems at Kuwait Investment Company, and afterwards spent two years as a senior technical consultant, evaluating loan management and customer information systems and supporting major telecommunications carriers.

From 2003 he served as Group Chief Information Officer of the Crescent Group of Companies, holding concurrent posts as Senior Vice President and Head of Information Technology at its commercial bank, Chief Operating Officer and Chief Information Officer at its telecommunications business, and Chief Information Officer at its steel operation, where he guided the group’s ERP selection and implementation. He then spent more than eight years as General Manager and Group Chief Information Officer of the Ghulam Faruque Group. His domain experience spans banking, telecommunications, trading, shipping, and manufacturing — sugar, cement, packaging, and ethanol. He holds the PMP and Microsoft Certified Systems Engineer certifications, with formal training in SAP Financials and Oracle Applications.

Lorie Bennett

Partner, Transactions

A graduate of Texas Woman's University and the University of North Texas, Lorie began her career outside financial services — selling dental science products at PerioSciences, where she ranked among the firm's top performers and lifted territory revenue by more than thirty percent in a single quarter. It was a consultative sale to skeptical professionals, and it set the pattern for everything that followed.

She moved into finance in 2013, first as a financial advisor at Edward Jones, then as a wealth advisor at United Capital, where she advised Fortune 500 retirees on retirement income and planning strategy. In late 2016 she joined Generational Equity as Operations Manager and spent six years inside M&A — establishing and administering the trust structures that hold sale proceeds, and coordinating advisors, bankers, and legal counsel across the full life of a transaction.

Two years at Farther followed, where she served as primary relationship manager for advisors and high-net-worth clients, before she joined Wealth Advisors Trust Company in 2025 as a Fiduciary Strategist, advising wealth advisors, estate attorneys, and CPAs on trust structures and fiduciary risk. She holds the Series 7 and Series 63, and completed the Cannon School's Essentials of Trusts program at Wealth Advisors Trust.

Jeff Chang

Advisor, Governance & Compliance

A graduate of Baruch College in 1981, Jeff worked his way through school — first in import/export at J.E. Maiyme, then as a clerk with the U.S. Post Office. Before turning to finance, he served as a New York City police officer from 1982 to 1984, an experience that shaped the discipline and judgment he would later bring to trading floors and compliance work.

After a bridge year at Wang Laboratories in Boston, Jeff broke onto the Street in 1985 at A.G. Becker Paribas, trading on the equities desk before being posted to the floor of the New York Stock Exchange. He went on to trade commodities at Collins Commodities on the New York Mercantile Exchange, then spent eleven years at Bear Stearns — first on the retail desk, then in trade processing, running clearing operations for small broker-dealers.

In 1997, Jeff moved to the supervisory side of the business as Head of Compliance at HGK Asset Management, a post he held for eight years. Few compliance officers can say they have stood where the rules are tested — on the exchange floor, at the trading desk, and in the clearing back office. Jeff has stood in all three.

Abe Shekel

Partner, Growth

Abe began his career in the heart of Wall Street at Phoenix Financial Services, working on private equity in SEC-registered funds. He went on to become a key builder at Farther, the fastest-growing advisory firm in the country, taking the RIA from seed through Series C. There he introduced a SaaS-based revenue stream, spinning data-driven prospecting out of Farther’s own technology and selling it to boutique RIAs across the country.

He developed key accounts for the digital recordkeeper 401GO, expanding across Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Cambridge Investment Research, Ameriprise, Osaic, LPL Financial, and Goldman Sachs among others, and built fund models specific to retirement investments. As a Partner at Advintro, he manages a curated portfolio of more than thirty companies in wealth, asset management, banking, and insurance. He positions fintech firms across financial planning, data analytics and reporting, organic growth, and estate management, and runs their go-to-market work — market exposure, benchmarking, sponsorships, and thought leadership.

His work has centered on building growth engines for financial firms — advisor books, enterprise wealth platform sales, and new revenue channels — including an intelligence platform built specifically for wealth managers, a playbook he is repeating in VCC’s RCM vertical. He is a regular at the New York Stock Exchange and NASDAQ, taking part in listings and bell ceremonies. He took bachelor’s degrees in General Management from EBS Universität für Wirtschaft und Recht and in Business Administration from the Goodman School of Business at Brock University. He holds the Series 7 and Series 63. He is also an Accredited Investment Fiduciary, a designation built around the highest standard of care.
